Bag of Words (BoW)

Definition

Bag of Words (BoW) is one of the simplest and most widely used Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques for converting text into numerical data. 
Since machine learning algorithms cannot process text directly, Bag of Words transforms text into a matrix of numbers based on the frequency of words.
The Bag of Words model counts how many times each word appears in a document without considering the grammar, sentence structure, or the order of words.

In simple words:
Bag of Words represents a document by counting the occurrence (frequency) of each word in the document.

Why is it called "Bag of Words"?

Imagine putting all the words from a sentence into a bag.

Sentence:

He is a good boy

The bag contains:

He
is
a
good
boy

If we rearrange the sentence:

Good boy is he

or

Boy is good he

Bag of Words treats all of them as the same because it only looks at the words present, not their order.

Example Dataset

Consider the following dataset:

Text                          Output
He is a good boy              1
She is a good girl            1
Boy and girl are good         1

The output column represents the target class. In this example, all sentences belong to the same class (1).

Step 1: Convert Text into Lowercase
Before processing, convert all sentences into lowercase.

Original Sentence
He is a good boy

Lowercase
he is a good boy

Similarly,
She is a good girl

becomes
she is a good girl

and
Boy and girl are good

becomes
boy and girl are good

Lowercasing ensures that words like Boy and boy are treated as the same word.

==========================================
Advantages of Bag of Words (BoW)
==========================================

1. Simple and Intuitive
   - Bag of Words is easy to understand and implement.
   - It converts text into numerical vectors using word frequency.

2. Fixed Size Input
   - Every document is converted into a fixed-length feature vector.
   - These vectors can be directly used by Machine Learning algorithms such as
     Logistic Regression, Naïve Bayes, Decision Trees, and SVM.

3. Easy to Implement
   - Available in Scikit-learn using CountVectorizer.
   - Requires very little preprocessing.

4. Fast Processing
   - Suitable for small and medium-sized datasets.
   - Computationally efficient compared to advanced embedding techniques.

5. Useful for Text Classification
   - Works well for tasks like Spam Detection, Sentiment Analysis,
     Document Classification, and News Classification.


==========================================
Disadvantages of Bag of Words (BoW)
==========================================

1. Sparse Matrix (High-Dimensional Data)
   - Large vocabularies create many columns.
   - Most values are 0, resulting in a sparse matrix.
   - Sparse matrices increase memory usage and may lead to overfitting.

2. Ignores Word Order
   - The sequence of words is not considered.
   - Example:
       "Dog bites man"
       "Man bites dog"
     Both sentences have the same Bag of Words representation,
     even though their meanings are different.

3. Out of Vocabulary (OOV)
   - Words that were not present during training cannot be represented.
   - Unknown words are simply ignored by the model.

4. Cannot Capture Semantic Meaning
   - Bag of Words only counts word frequency.
   - It does not understand the meaning or context of words.
   - Example:
       "The food is good"
       "The food is not good"
     Both sentences appear very similar because BoW does not understand
     that the word "not" changes the sentiment.

5. No Context Awareness
   - Words with multiple meanings are treated the same.
   - Example:
       "Bank" (river bank)
       "Bank" (financial institution)
     BoW cannot distinguish between these meanings.

6. Treats Similar Words as Different
   - Synonyms are considered different words.
   - Example:
       Car, Automobile, Vehicle
     These are treated as separate features even though they have similar meanings.
