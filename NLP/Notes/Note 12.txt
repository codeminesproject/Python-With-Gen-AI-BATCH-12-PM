
==============================================================
           WORD2VEC TRAINING PROCESS (EPOCHS)
==============================================================

1. EXAMPLE DATASET
--------------------------------------------------------------

Suppose the training dataset contains only 5 sentences.

+----+-----------------------------------------+
| No | Sentence                                |
+----+-----------------------------------------+
| 1  | The king is a strong man.               |
| 2  | The queen is a wise woman.              |
| 3  | The prince is young.                    |
| 4  | The princess is beautiful.              |
| 5  | The king ruled the kingdom.             |
+----+-----------------------------------------+

Initial embedding of the word "king"

+------+-------+-------+-------+-------+
| Word |  D1   |  D2   |  D3   |  D4   |
+------+-------+-------+-------+-------+
| king | 0.20  | 0.50  | -0.10 | 0.80  |
+------+-------+-------+-------+-------+

==============================================================
2. EPOCH 1 (FIRST PASS)
==============================================================

The model reads all 5 sentences once.

Sentence 1 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 2 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 3 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 4 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 5 --> Update Embedding

Embedding after Epoch 1

+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| Word |  D1   |  D2   | D3   | D4   |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| king | 0.32  | 0.48  | 0.05 | 0.82 |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+

==============================================================
3. EPOCH 2 (SECOND PASS)
==============================================================

The model again starts from Sentence 1.

Sentence 1 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 2 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 3 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 4 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 5 --> Update Embedding

Embedding after Epoch 2

+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| Word |  D1   |  D2   | D3   | D4   |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| king | 0.51  | 0.62  | 0.38 | 0.74 |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+

==============================================================
4. EPOCH 3 (THIRD PASS)
==============================================================

The model again processes all 5 sentences.

Sentence 1 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 2 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 3 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 4 --> Update Embedding
Sentence 5 --> Update Embedding

Embedding after Epoch 3

+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| Word |  D1   |  D2   | D3   | D4   |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| king | 0.72  | 0.75  | 0.59 | 0.71 |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+

==============================================================
5. AFTER MANY EPOCHS
==============================================================

The embedding gradually stabilizes.

+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| Word |  D1   |  D2   | D3   | D4   |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+
| king | 0.92  | 0.81  | 0.74 | 0.66 |
+------+-------+-------+------+------+

==============================================================
6. WHAT IS AN EPOCH?
==============================================================

An Epoch = One complete pass through the entire training dataset.

Example:

Dataset = 1000 sentences

Epoch 1
--------
Sentence 1
Sentence 2
...
Sentence 1000

Epoch 2
--------
Again starts from Sentence 1 and processes all 1000 sentences.

==============================================================
7. TYPICAL NUMBER OF EPOCHS
==============================================================

+----------------------+--------------+
| Dataset Size         | Epochs       |
+----------------------+--------------+
| Small Dataset        | 20 - 100     |
| Medium Dataset       | 10 - 30      |
| Large Dataset        | 5 - 10       |
+----------------------+--------------+

==============================================================
8. GOOGLE'S ORIGINAL WORD2VEC
==============================================================

Training Corpus : 100+ Billion Words
Embedding Size  : 300
Window Size     : 5
Training Epochs : About 5

==============================================================
9. HOW MANY UPDATES HAPPEN?
==============================================================

Example:

1000 Sentences
× 10 Words per Sentence
= 10,000 Words

20 Epochs

10,000 × 20 = 200,000 Word Updates

The embedding matrix (hidden layer) is updated after every
training example.

==============================================================
10. INTERVIEW ANSWER
==============================================================

Q. How many times are Word2Vec embeddings updated?

Answer:
The embeddings are updated after every training example.
One complete pass through the dataset is called an epoch.
The model repeats this process for multiple epochs until
training is complete or the embeddings converge.
