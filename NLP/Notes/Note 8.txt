WORD EMBEDDING

Definition

Word Embedding is a technique in Natural Language Processing (NLP) that
converts words into numerical vectors (decimal numbers) so that a
computer can understand the meaning and relationship between words.

Unlike Bag of Words (BoW) or TF-IDF, Word Embedding captures the
semantic meaning of words. Words with similar meanings have similar
vector representations.

Example

Sentence: 
The cat is sitting on the mat.
The dog is eating meat.

Example Word Vectors

Word Vector (Example)

cat [0.21, -0.45, 0.78] 
dog [0.24, -0.41, 0.75] 
mat [0.81, 0.12, -0.33]
sitting [0.55, 0.63, 0.18]

Observation

-   “cat” and “dog” have similar vector values because both are animals.
-   “cat” and “mat” have different vectors because their meanings are
    different.

Key Points

1.  Converts words into dense numerical vectors.
2.  Captures the semantic meaning of words.
3.  Similar words have similar vector representations.
4.  Used in modern NLP models such as Word2Vec, GloVe, FastText, and BERT.


Word Embeddings are divided into two main approaches:

1. Count / Frequency Based Methods

These methods represent words based on how frequently they occur in a
document.

Methods: 
1. One Hot Encoding (OHE) 
2. Bag of Words (BoW) 
3. TF-IDF (TermFrequency – Inverse Document Frequency)

2. Prediction / Deep Learning Based Methods

These methods use Artificial Neural Networks (ANN) to learn the meaning
of words.

Deep Learning Model: Word2Vec

Word2Vec has two architectures: 
1. CBOW (Continuous Bag of Words) 
2. Skip-Gram
