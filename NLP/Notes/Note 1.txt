Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a branch of Artificial Intelligence (AI) that enables 
computers to understand, interpret, analyze, and generate human language 
(such as English, Hindi, French, etc.) in a meaningful way.

NLP combines Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Linguistics to help computers process 
and understand natural language.

Example
Input (Human Language): I love learning Natural Language Processing.

NLP Processing
Step 1: Tokenize the sentence into words.
Step 2: Remove unnecessary words (stopwords).
Step 3: Identify the part of speech of each word.
Step 4: Understand the meaning of the sentence.
Step 5: Perform sentiment analysis.

Sentiment: Positive

Tokenization

It is a process of converting paragraph into words (token)

Step 1: Corpus (Input)
Step 2: Document (Convert copus (paragraphs) into sentences)
step 3: words (Convert sentences into words with stopword)
step 4: token (unique words without stopwords)

------------------------------------------------------------------------

For tokenization we required a python library NLTK - Natural Language Toolkit

Step 1: install nltk package - pip install nltk
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stemming
Stemming is the process of reducing a word to its word stem that affixes to suffixes and prefixes 
or to the roots of words known as a lemma. Stemming is important in natural language understanding (NLU) 
and natural language processing (NLP).

Type of Stemming:
1. PorterStemmer
2. RegexpStemmer
3. SnowballStemmer

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Lemmatization
Lemmatization technique is like stemming. 
The output we will get after lemmatization is called ‘lemma’, which is a root word rather than root stem, 
the output of stemming. After lemmatization, we will be getting a valid word that means the same thing.
NLTK provides WordNetLemmatizer class which is a thin wrapper around the wordnet corpus. 
This class uses morphy() function to the WordNet CorpusReader class to find a lemma.

usecases:
Q&A,chatbots,text summarization

Stopwords

Stopwords are commonly used words in a language that usually do not add significant meaning to the text.

Examples:
is, am, are, the, a, an, of, in, on, for, with, and, to

Part of Speech Tagging

Part-of-Speech (POS) Tagging is the process of identifying and assigning the correct grammatical 
category (part of speech) to each word in a sentence based on its meaning and context.
These grammatical categories include noun, pronoun, verb, adjective, adverb, preposition, conjunction, 
determiner, interjection, and others.

POS Tags in NLP (NLTK)

NN   - Noun
       Example: book, car

NNS  - Plural Noun
       Example: books

NNP  - Proper Noun
       Example: Mumbai, India

VB   - Verb (Base Form)
       Example: eat

VBD  - Verb (Past Tense)
       Example: ate

VBG  - Verb (Present Participle / Gerund)
       Example: eating

VBN  - Verb (Past Participle)
       Example: eaten

VBP  - Verb (Present Tense)
       Example: eat

VBZ  - Verb (3rd Person Singular Present)
       Example: eats

JJ   - Adjective
       Example: beautiful

JJR  - Comparative Adjective
       Example: bigger

JJS  - Superlative Adjective
       Example: biggest

RB   - Adverb
       Example: quickly

DT   - Determiner
       Example: the, a, an

PRP  - Personal Pronoun
       Example: he, she, they

IN   - Preposition
       Example: in, on, at

CC   - Coordinating Conjunction
       Example: and, but, or

UH   - Interjection
       Example: wow!, oh!


Why is POS Tagging Important?
POS tagging helps computers understand the grammatical structure of a sentence. 

It is widely used in:
Machine Translation
Text Summarization
Question Answering Systems
Chatbots
Sentiment Analysis
Named Entity Recognition (NER)
Information Extraction
Speech Recognition

Sentence:
Rahul is playing cricket.

POS Tags:

Word        POS Tag     Meaning
-----------------------------------------------
Rahul       NNP         Proper Noun
is          VBZ         Verb (3rd Person Singular Present)
playing     VBG         Verb (Present Participle)
cricket     NN          Noun
.           .           Punctuation

Named Entity Recognition

Named Entity Recognition (NER) is a Natural Language Processing (NLP) technique that identifies 
and classifies important entities (real-world objects) mentioned in a text into predefined categories 
such as Person, Organization, Location, Date, Time, Money, Percentage, Email, Product, and more.
